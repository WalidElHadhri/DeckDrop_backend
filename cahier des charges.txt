AR - DECKDROP Website Project Description (Full Specification)
1. Project Overview
Website Name: AR - DECKDROP
 Domain: AR - DECKDROP.de
 Type of Website: E-Commerce Online Shop (Trading Card Games + Accessories + Anime/Gaming Merchandise)
 Target Market: Germany and Europe
 Languages: German and English (Language switch is mandatory)
AR - DECKDROP is an online store focused on selling sealed Trading Card Game (TCG) products and related merchandise. The store will mainly offer products for popular card games such as Pokémon TCG, Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG, and One Piece TCG. Additionally, the store will sell TCG accessories, anime figures, and gaming/anime merchandise.
The main goal of the website is to provide a modern, premium, user-friendly shopping experience, with a clean and luxurious design that is colorful and matches the brand logo style.

2. Website Goals and Functions
The website should allow customers to:
Browse products easily by category
Search products quickly using a search bar
View product details with clear descriptions and images
Add products to wishlist/favorites
Add products to cart and checkout smoothly
Place orders as guest users or registered users
Create an account, manage addresses, and view order history
Track orders using tracking links (DHL)
Place and pay for preorders before release date
Read blog/news posts about upcoming products and preorders
The website must be professional and trustworthy to support online payments and build customer confidence.

3. Target Audience
The target audience includes:
TCG players and collectors (Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece)
Anime and gaming fans
Customers mainly located in Germany, but also across Europe
People interested in sealed products, collectible items, and accessories

4. Product Categories
The main product categories of the store are:
Pokémon TCG
Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG
One Piece TCG
TCG Accessories (sleeves, binders, deck boxes, playmats, etc.)
Figures (anime and gaming figures)
Merchandise (anime/gaming merch such as posters, clothing, collectibles)
Each category should have its own page and subcategory structure if needed.

The design should look:
Modern
